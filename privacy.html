Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Important: This policy should be reviewed whenever forms, analytics, payments, scheduling, storage or client portals change.

1. Who operates this website

Ederito is a digital services brand operated by Zanara Labs LLC. Questions may be sent to contact@ederito.com.

2. Information we collect

We may collect information you voluntarily submit, including your name, business name, email address, phone number, service request, budget, timeline, project details and review content. Do not submit Social Security numbers, banking credentials, passwords or other highly sensitive information through public forms.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, prepare proposals, communicate about services, manage projects, provide support, prevent misuse and improve the website.

4. Email-based forms

The current forms prepare an email in the visitor’s email application. Information is not automatically stored by this static website unless a future database, form provider or client portal is connected.

5. Service providers

Third-party services may be used for hosting, payments, analytics, email, file storage, scheduling or project management. Those providers may process information under their own privacy terms.

6. Reviews

Reviews are published only after review and with the permission provided by the submitting customer. Obvious spelling or formatting issues may be corrected without changing the meaning.

7. Retention and security

Information is retained only as reasonably needed for inquiries, projects, legal obligations and business records. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of information previously provided by contacting contact@ederito.com, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements.

9. Updates

This policy may be updated as services and technology change. The effective date will be revised when material changes are made.
