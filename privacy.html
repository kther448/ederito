Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Important: This policy should be reviewed whenever forms, analytics, payments, scheduling, storage or client portals change.

1. Who operates this website

Ederito is a digital services brand operated by Zanara Labs LLC. Questions may be sent to contact@ederito.com.

2. Information we collect

We may collect information you voluntarily submit, including your name, business name, email address, phone number, service request, budget, timeline, project details, rating and review content. Do not submit Social Security numbers, banking credentials, passwords or other highly sensitive information through public forms.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, prepare proposals, communicate about services, manage projects, provide support, prevent misuse, display authorized client reviews and improve the website.

4. Website forms

The project request form prepares an email in the visitor’s email application. The public review form sends the submitted review to a protected database so it can appear directly on the website. Review submissions include the reviewer’s name, optional business name, private email address, rating, review text and submission time.

5. Service providers

The website may use third-party services for hosting, database storage, security, payments, analytics, email, file storage, scheduling or project management. The public review system currently uses Supabase for database and API services. Those providers may process information under their own privacy terms.

6. Public reviews

When a reviewer gives permission and publishes a review, the reviewer’s name, optional business name, rating, review text and review date become publicly visible on the Ederito website. The reviewer’s email address is not displayed publicly and is used to help limit repeated submissions, manage misuse and respond to privacy requests. Do not place private contact, payment or account information inside the public review text.

7. Retention and security

Information is retained only as reasonably needed for inquiries, projects, public reviews, misuse prevention, legal obligations and business records. Access controls and database policies are used to prevent public access to review email addresses, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

You may request access, correction, removal or deletion of information previously provided by contacting contact@ederito.com, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements. A published review may be removed from public display after a verified request.

9. Updates

This policy may be updated as services and technology change. The effective date will be revised when material changes are made.
